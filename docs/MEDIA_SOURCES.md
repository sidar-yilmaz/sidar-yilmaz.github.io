Xwebun's name was supplied by the website owner. Its 30-degree rotor tilt and interchangeable interaction tips are described in the physical-interaction paper's experimental platform section. SMORS' three rigid and three compliant arms, full actuation, and jumping/flying locomotion are described in its supplied manuscript. Tool changing through platform motion at a base station, ball-lock coupling, and rotary power transfer are described in the supplied thesis.

No publication status or authorship was inferred for the anonymized manuscripts. The existing published ICUAS entry remains sourced from Scholar.
